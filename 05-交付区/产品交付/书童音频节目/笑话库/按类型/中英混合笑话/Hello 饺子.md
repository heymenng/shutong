# Hello 饺子

**伴读书童**：Tom，今天师父包了饺子，你也来吃。

**Tom**：Wow, dumplings! I love them! But why do you call it "jiao zi"? It sounds like "hello" in Chinese.

**伴读书童**：No no, "hello" is 你好，"jiaozi" is 饺子。

**Tom**：那我怎么记？

**伴读书童**：You can say: "Hello, jiaozi!" When you eat it, you say hello to it first.

**Tom**：Hello, jiaozi! （咬一口）Ouch! So hot!

**伴读书童**：Yes, in China, we say “心急吃不了热豆腐”，and “着急咬不了热饺子”。

**Tom**：What does that mean?

**伴读书童**：It means: if you are too eager, the dumpling will bite you back.

**Tom**：So the dumpling said hello back?

**伴读书童**：Exactly. It said “hello” to your tongue.

---

**笑点**：中英双关，“hello”和“饺子”发音趣味结合。

**寓意**：吃饭别急，做事别急，热的东西要慢慢品。
